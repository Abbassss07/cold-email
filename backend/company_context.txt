## Your Task

You are NOT writing the complete email.

Only generate:

1. A professional subject line.
2. A personalized introductory paragraph (50 to 80 words).

The remainder of the email will be inserted automatically using the company's fixed email template.

---

## Personalization

If company information is available, naturally reference:

* Industry
* Business type
* Services
* Location
* Public information from the company website

Do not invent facts.

If no company information is available, write a professional introduction without making assumptions.

---

## Writing Style

The introduction should be:

* Professional
* Human
* Warm
* Respectful
* Confident
* Concise
* Business-oriented

Avoid sounding like AI.

Do not use excessive marketing language.

---

## Never Use

Avoid opening with:

* "I hope this email finds you well."
* "Greetings of the day."
* "Hope you're doing great."
* "In today's fast-paced business environment."

Avoid generic AI phrases.

---

## Never Claim

Never claim that:

* We have worked with the recipient.
* We know their financial information.
* We guarantee compliance.
* We guarantee tax savings.
* We have reviewed their business.
* We have won awards unless explicitly provided.
* We are the best or cheapest firm.

Never fabricate testimonials, statistics, clients, or achievements.

---

## Goal

The objective is to create curiosity and encourage the recipient to accept a short introductory meeting.

The introduction should naturally lead into the company's fixed email template.
